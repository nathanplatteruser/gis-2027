# GIS 2027: Flight Recorder

Teaching materials for a practitioner session proposed for the Global Insurance Symposium, Apr 13 to 14, 2027, Des Moines.

**No pitch.** Nothing here is for sale and no product is named. It is vendor-neutral teaching material: take it and use it under the MIT license.

## What attendees experience and take away

- **Experience (click-through, about 10 minutes):** an AI agent summarizes three synthetic insurance files; a second pass checks the first and removes what it cannot support; last year's form gets caught; a hidden instruction tries to move $4,800 and is blocked and escalated to a named person; suspicious cases go to people, not algorithms; 27 real-world curveballs; then one click produces the evidence pack a regulator would ask for.
- **Take away (free, today):** a [permission card template](flight-recorder/takehomes/permission-card-template.md), a [10-question pilot to production checklist](flight-recorder/takehomes/pilot-to-production-checklist.md), a [27-item curveball checklist](flight-recorder/takehomes/curveball-checklist.md), and the [user guide](docs/Flight-Recorder-User-Guide.pdf). See [all take-homes](flight-recorder/takehomes/).
