# Pilot to Production: 10 questions before an AI workflow goes live

Answer yes to all ten, or write down who accepted the risk and why.

1. **Owner.** Is a named business role accountable for this workflow, with a signed permission card?
2. **Scope.** Is there a written "never allowed" list, and does the system enforce it, not just state it?
3. **Sources.** Does every output sentence point to the line it came from?
4. **Second pass.** Does something separate from the first pass check the output and remove what it can't support?
5. **Currency.** Does it check that forms, riders, guidelines, and treaty wordings are the current edition before using them?
6. **Hidden instructions.** Have you tested what happens when a document contains instructions aimed at the AI?
7. **Money and contact.** Is it technically impossible for the agent to move money or contact a customer without a human?
8. **Escalation.** When something is blocked, does a named person get it, with a response time?
9. **Ledger.** Is every read, draft, check, block, and human decision logged with time and actor?
10. **Evidence on request.** Could you hand a regulator the evidence pack for any file within a day?

Mapped to: NAIC AI Model Bulletin (Dec 4, 2023), Section 3 AIS Program Guidelines 1.0 to 4.0 and Section 4 Regulatory Oversight and Examination Considerations.
